Dengarkan dan baca percakapan Rini & Budi di pagi hari. Klik ikon 🔊 untuk mendengarkan.
Ri
"Good morning, Budi! How are you today?"
Bu
"Good morning, Rini! I'm a little tired, but I'm okay."
Ri
"Did you work late yesterday?"
Bu
"Yes, I worked until nine o'clock. What about you?"
Ri
"I went home early. I cooked dinner for my family."
Bu
"That sounds nice. See you at the office!"
tired: lelah/capek
worked late: bekerja lembur/sampai larut
cooked dinner: memasak makan malam
sounds nice: kedengarannya menyenangkan

Kisah Inspiratif: Sari's Success

Sari is a 38-year-old woman. She used to work as a factory worker. Now, she has a small souvenir shop in Malioboro. Sari learned English at PKBM Tunas Mandiri. At first, she thought English was very difficult. She could not understand what foreign tourists said. However, she practiced every day with her friends and teachers.

Today, Sari can serve customers from other countries easily. She can explain the price, the materials of the souvenirs, and give directions. Her business is growing because tourists feel comfortable buying from her. Sari says, "Learning English opens new doors for my life."

Kosakata: factory worker (buruh pabrik), foreign tourists (turis asing), serve (melayani), materials (bahan), directions (arah).
Soal Pemahaman
1. Where does Sari have her shop?
2. What did she think about English at first?
3. Who did she practice English with?
4. Why is her business growing?
5. What does learning English do for her life?
