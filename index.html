Urutan pilihanmu:

Teks 1: Pengumuman

📋 Teks 1: Community Announcement
Community Announcement

Dear Residents of RW 05,

We would like to inform you that there will be a community clean-up activity this Sunday, June 8, 2026, starting at 07.00 AM.

All residents are kindly requested to participate. Please bring your own cleaning tools such as brooms and trash bags.

After the clean-up, we will have a short meeting to discuss the community garden project.

Thank you for your cooperation.

Best regards,
The Neighborhood Committee
📖 Kosakata Baru
inform = memberitahukan
residents = warga/penghuni

Teks 2: My Daily Routine

📄 Teks 2: Teks Informasi Singkat
My Daily Routine

My name is Dewi. I am 32 years old and I live in Yogyakarta. I work at a small food stall near my house from Monday to Saturday.

Every morning, I wake up at four-thirty. I cook rice and prepare breakfast for my family before I go to work. I open the food stall at six o'clock.

At noon, I take a short break and eat lunch with my husband. In the evening, after closing the stall, I attend English classes at PKBM Tunas Mandiri. I study English because I want to communicate better with customers and improve my skills.

I believe that learning is important at any age. I am proud to keep studying even though I am busy every day.

📖 Kosakata Baru
routine = rutinitas
prepare = menyiapkan
attend = menghadiri/mengikuti
communicate = berkomunikasi
improve = meningkatkan
proud = bangga
