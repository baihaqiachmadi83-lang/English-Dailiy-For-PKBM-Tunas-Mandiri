Kalimat Negatif di Rumah ❌

Bagaimana cara bilang "TIDAK" dalam bahasa Inggris? Kita pakai don't atau doesn't!

He watches TV every night.
✅ Positif: Subjek + Kata Kerja (+S/ES untuk He/She/It)
DON'T
I / You / We / They
They don't watch TV.
DOESN'T
He / She / It
He doesn't watch TV.
