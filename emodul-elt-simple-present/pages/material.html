🎯 Apa itu Simple Present Tense?

Simple Present Tense adalah tenses yang kamu pakai untuk cerita tentang:

  • ✅ Kebiasaan sehari-hari - Contoh: "I wake up at 6 AM every day"
  • ✅ Fakta umum - Contoh: "The sun rises in the east"
  • ✅ Jadwal tetap - Contoh: "School starts at 7 AM"
  • ✅ Hobi & kemampuan - Contoh: "She plays guitar very well"
💡 Tips: Simple Present itu seperti cerita rutinitas kamu!

🔧 Formula Explorer - Klik untuk Expand!

✅ Positive Form

Subject + Verb1 (s/es) + Object

Klik untuk lihat contoh!

Contoh:

• I study English every day.

• She plays badminton.

• They like pizza.

📝 Ingat: tambah -s/es untuk He/She/It!

❌ Negative Form

Subject + do/does + not + Verb1

Klik untuk lihat contoh!

Contoh:

• I don't like spicy food.

• He doesn't play football.

• We don't watch TV.

📝 Don't = do not, Doesn't = does not

❓ Question Form

Do/Does + Subject + Verb1 + ?

Klik untuk lihat contoh!

Contoh:

• Do you speak English?

• Does she study here?

• Do they like music?

📝 Pakai 'Does' untuk He/She/It, 'Do' untuk yang lain
