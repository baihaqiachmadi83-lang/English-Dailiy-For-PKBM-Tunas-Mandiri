2

Kata Tanya (WH-Questions) 🔤

Klik setiap kartu untuk melihat contoh kalimat di lingkungan kesehatan!

WHERE
Di mana?
Where does the doctor work?
✅ She works at the clinic.
WHEN
Kapan?
When does the clinic open?
✅ It opens at 8 AM.
WHAT
Apa?
What does the nurse do?
✅ She checks the patients.
WHO
Siapa?
Who works at the hospital?
✅ The doctors and nurses work there.
HOW
Bagaimana?
How do they help patients?
✅ They give medicine and advice.
WHY
Mengapa?
Why do people go to the clinic?
✅ Because they feel sick.
