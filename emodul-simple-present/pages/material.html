01

📚 Pengertian Simple Present Tense

Simple Present Tense adalah salah satu dari 16 tenses dalam bahasa Inggris yang paling sering digunakan. Tense ini digunakan untuk menyatakan kegiatan atau kejadian yang terjadi pada saat ini (present), kebiasaan (habitual action), dan fakta umum (general truth).

💡 Kapan Menggunakan Simple Present?

  • Untuk menyatakan kebiasaan atau rutinitas sehari-hari
  • Untuk menyatakan fakta umum atau kebenaran universal
  • Untuk menyatakan jadwal yang tetap atau terjadwal
  • Untuk menyatakan kemampuan atau karakteristik seseorang
02

📝 Rumus Simple Present Tense

Positive

Kalimat Positif

Subject + Verb 1 (s/es) + Object

Contoh:

  • I study English every day.
  • She likes chocolate.
  • They play football on Sunday.
  • He works at a hospital.
Catatan: Tambahkan s atau es untuk subject He, She, It
Negative

Kalimat Negatif

Subject + do/does + not + Verb 1 + Object

Contoh:

  • I do not (don't) study at night.
  • She does not (doesn't) like vegetables.
  • They do not (don't) play basketball.
  • He does not (doesn't) work on weekends.
Catatan: Gunakan does not untuk He, She, It dan do not untuk yang lainnya
Interrogative

Kalimat Tanya

Do/Does + Subject + Verb 1 + Object + ?

Contoh:

  • Do you study English? - Yes, I do / No, I don't
  • Does she like coffee? - Yes, she does / No, she doesn't
  • Do they play tennis? - Yes, they do / No, they don't
  • Does he work here? - Yes, he does / No, he doesn't
Catatan: Gunakan Does untuk He, She, It dan Do untuk yang lainnya

📊 Tabel Subject & Auxiliary Verb

Subject Auxiliary (Positive) Auxiliary (Negative) Auxiliary (Question) Verb
I - do not / don't Do Verb 1 (tanpa s/es)
You - do not / don't Do Verb 1 (tanpa s/es)
We - do not / don't Do Verb 1 (tanpa s/es)
They - do not / don't Do Verb 1 (tanpa s/es)
He - does not / doesn't Does Verb 1 + s/es
She - does not / doesn't Does Verb 1 + s/es
It - does not / doesn't Does Verb 1 + s/es

03

🎯 Fungsi & Penggunaan

🔁

Kebiasaan / Rutinitas

Menyatakan kegiatan yang dilakukan secara berulang atau rutin.

✅ I wake up at 6 AM every day.

✅ She exercises three times a week.

✅ We have dinner together every night.

🌍

Fakta Umum

Menyatakan kebenaran umum atau fakta ilmiah.

✅ The sun rises in the east.

✅ Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.

✅ Cats love fish.

📅

Jadwal Tetap

Menyatakan jadwal atau agenda yang sudah ditentukan.

✅ The train leaves at 8 PM.

✅ The movie starts at 7 o'clock.

✅ School begins in September.

⭐

Kemampuan & Karakteristik

Menyatakan kemampuan atau sifat seseorang/sesuatu.

✅ He speaks three languages.

✅ My sister plays the piano well.

✅ Dogs bark loudly.

🌌

⏰ Time Signals (Kata Keterangan Waktu)

Time signals atau kata keterangan waktu adalah kata-kata yang menunjukkan kapan suatu kegiatan dilakukan. Berikut adalah time signals yang sering digunakan dengan Simple Present Tense:

Frequency Adverbs

  • always (selalu)
  • usually (biasanya)
  • often (sering)
  • sometimes (kadang-kadang)
  • seldom (jarang)
  • rarely (jarang sekali)
  • never (tidak pernah)

Time Expressions

  • every day (setiap hari)
  • every week (setiap minggu)
  • every month (setiap bulan)
  • every year (setiap tahun)
  • once a week (seminggu sekali)
  • twice a day (dua kali sehari)
  • on Mondays (setiap Senin)

💡 Posisi Time Signal dalam Kalimat:

I always brush my teeth before bed.

Frequency adverb biasanya ditempatkan sebelum verb utama

She goes to the gym every day.

Time expression biasanya ditempatkan di akhir kalimat

05

✏️ Verb Forms (Bentuk Kata Kerja)

Dalam Simple Present Tense, kata kerja (verb) mengalami perubahan bentuk tergantung pada subject. Untuk subject He, She, It, kita harus menambahkan -s atau -es pada verb.

📏 Aturan Penambahan -s/-es:

1. Tambahkan -s pada kebanyakan verb

Base Form + s/es Contoh Kalimat
work works He works hard.
play plays She plays piano.
read reads It reads well.

2. Tambahkan -es jika verb berakhiran: -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z, -o

Base Form + s/es Contoh Kalimat
watch watches He watches TV.
go goes She goes to school.
wash washes Mom washes the dishes.
fix fixes He fixes computers.

3. Ubah -y menjadi -ies jika sebelumnya konsonan

Base Form + s/es Contoh Kalimat
study studies She studies hard.
cry cries The baby cries loudly.
fly flies The bird flies high.

⚠️ Jika sebelum -y adalah vokal (a,e,i,o,u), cukup tambahkan -s: play → plays, say → says

4. Irregular Verbs (Kata Kerja Tidak Beraturan)

Base Form + s/es Contoh Kalimat
have has She has a car.
be is/am/are He is a student.

06

📝 Contoh Kalimat Lengkap

✅ Kalimat Positif (Positive Sentences)

  1. I drink coffee every morning.
  2. She teaches English at the university.
  3. They live in Jakarta.
  4. He drives to work every day.
  5. We enjoy watching movies together.
  6. The sun sets in the west.
  7. My mom cooks delicious food.

❌ Kalimat Negatif (Negative Sentences)

  1. I don't eat meat.
  2. She doesn't like spicy food.
  3. They don't play video games.
  4. He doesn't smoke.
  5. We don't watch TV on weekdays.
  6. The shop doesn't open on Sundays.
  7. My brother doesn't drink coffee.

❓ Kalimat Tanya (Interrogative Sentences)

  1. Do you speak English? → Yes, I do.
  2. Does she know the answer? → No, she doesn't.
  3. Do they have a pet? → Yes, they do.
  4. Does he work here? → No, he doesn't.
  5. Do we need tickets? → Yes, we do.
  6. Does it rain a lot here? → No, it doesn't.
  7. Do you like pizza? → Yes, I do!
